# Catalog sources — 25 October 2026

The shoe catalog uses original product photographs from Reebok, Steve Madden and Nine West, plus Shoe Palace and Sneakersnstuff. These are brand/retailer sources, not Chinese marketplaces. Existing non-shoe sources remain Reebok, ColourPop, Steve Madden, Herschel and Timex.

CATALOG-SOURCES.json records all 42 products, original listing and variant IDs, offers, 257 unique photos, download URLs, dimensions, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes. The 30 shoes alternate men’s and women’s styles. Their photos were requested through the source Shopify CDN with supported WebP/width parameters, verified locally, and bundled with the site. Color changes use photos from that exact color listing; unavailable colorways are omitted. No artificial recoloring or invented product reviews are used. A low-scoring denim Gritt colorway was excluded.

Shoes: 4,000–10,000 ETB, only three models at or above 9,500 ETB. Other categories are unchanged. Source USD prices use 161.874143 ETB/USD. Sneakersnstuff prices are EUR, using 181.34558068982824 ETB/EUR derived from the same dated rate snapshot (0.892628 EUR/USD). Round to whole ETB. Shipping, tax, import and delivery charges are excluded. Source availability does not establish local inventory. Source reviews are genuine published aggregates and may be shared across colorways; no stars appear without a checked score.

All source links stay in maintainer records. The customer UI has no external store links. Source photography and brand identification do not establish image licensing permission, authorized dealer status, physical product authenticity or local stock.
