# Shoe image sources

The storefront bundles photographs locally so they do not depend on blocked image hosts or hotlinks. No images were recolored. No Amazon listing prices, ratings, stock, model names, brand authorization, or authenticity were verified. No review stars or invented customer reviews are displayed. The ETB prices are shop prices selected within the owner's requested 3,500–6,000 ETB range; they are not currency conversions or claims about supplier costs. Descriptive product titles are not verified manufacturer model names. Confirm product sourcing, usage rights, real stock, exact models, sizes and authenticity before accepting customer payments.

Additional Anon files: sports-2.jpg (black/yellow utility high), sports-3.jpg (black/grey Nike high), sports-4.jpg (black/yellow technical sport shoe), shoe-1.jpg and shoe-1_1.jpg (burgundy/black formal shoe, two views), shoe-3.jpg (black ankle shoe), shoe-4.jpg and shoe-5.jpg (brown boot, two views).
