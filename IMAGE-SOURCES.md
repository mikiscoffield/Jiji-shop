# Shoe image sources

The storefront bundles photographs locally so they do not depend on blocked image hosts or hotlinks. No images were recolored. No Amazon listing prices, ratings, stock, model names, brand authorization, or authenticity were verified. No review stars or invented customer reviews are displayed. The ETB prices are shop prices selected within the owner's requested 3,500–6,000 ETB range; they are not currency conversions or claims about supplier costs. Descriptive product titles are not verified manufacturer model names. Confirm product sourcing, usage rights, real stock, exact models, sizes and authenticity before accepting customer payments.

Photographs show Nike-branded styles. Their availability from Jiji Shop is not independently verified.

The reference project's naming is not reliable brand or model identification. We identify visible brand marks, avoid copying its made-up model names, ratings and statistics, and do not imply Nike, Adidas, or New Balance affiliation. Public availability of reference assets is not independent clearance of underlying brand photography; obtain retailer-approved imagery or replace with your own product photography for a production business.

Additional Anon files: sports-2.jpg (black/yellow utility high), sports-3.jpg (black/grey Nike high), sports-4.jpg (black/yellow technical sport shoe), shoe-1.jpg and shoe-1_1.jpg (burgundy/black formal shoe, two views), shoe-3.jpg (black ankle shoe), shoe-4.jpg and shoe-5.jpg (brown boot, two views). Unclear brands are labeled Style edit rather than assigned an invented brand or model. Six of the ten additions are mainly black; the remaining styles retain their photographed colors.

Restored clothes, backpack and ring photographs are from https://github.com/keikaavousi/fake-store-api, public/img. Their MIT license is retained at public/images/lifestyle/FAKESTORE-LICENSE.txt. These photos were recovered from the original Jiji Shop commit rather than newly replaced with drawn placeholders.
